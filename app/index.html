FIELD NOTES

Your world starts here.

Paint in either view. The map and terrain update together.

Raise / Lower
Drag to build or carve terrain. Blend applies one controlled layer per stroke; release to add another.
Buildup
Blend prevents overlapping dabs from stacking. Stroke height sets the depth at full strength. Additive keeps building while you drag or hold.
Flatten
Uses the height under the first click as its target.
Smooth
Soften ridges. Hold Shift while sculpting.
Meld · M
Use a broad round brush to break down peaks and fill gaps together. Size controls the blending reach; Strength controls the speed. Hold to continue melding. Shift temporarily uses fine Smooth.
Stamp
Place a single peak with your selected heightmap.
Rivers
Select River and draw a rough route. On release, the channel is carved downhill with water and riverbank colors. Undo restores the terrain. Recarve channels reopens paths after later sculpting.
Objects
O opens trees, shrubs and rocks. Scatter splatters a brush area; Detail places one object per click. Eraser or Shift deletes objects in the circle, with an optional selected-type filter. Size and variation control the models. Objects follow sculpted terrain and are saved with history.
Sun / rotation
Sun azimuth and height light both views. Rotate the 2D map or orbit the 3D view using the sliders in View rotation. Painting, panning, and zooming follow the rotated view.
3D distance
Open 3D view and adjust Render distance to load terrain around the focus. Lower values are faster; the maximum renders the entire map and grows with its borders. Object capacity is one object per map sample.
Side panels
Click a section heading to fold it, or use Collapse all / Expand all. Panel choices and render distance are remembered in this browser. Undo/Redo stay above the world controls.
Move around
Space + drag pans. Right-drag orbits in 3D. Scroll zooms.
Brush size
[ and ] change the radius.
Brush preview
Turn on the translucent stamp footprint in both views. Preview opacity controls its visibility without changing paint strength. Its shape follows the selected heightmap and rotation.
History
Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo and redo. Click a history row to travel back.
Biomes
Pick from terrain samples the dominant painted biome or matches natural terrain; Match a color selects the nearest biome color. Brush strength goes down to 0.1%. B paints the selected biome. Water paints a blue surface color for lakes or rivers; use River to carve a physical channel. E or Shift restores natural colors. Snow and rock follow the terrain slope.
Reset
Reset views repairs the display. Reset world clears or restores terrain, rivers, paint and objects, with Undo support.
Save
Ctrl+S saves a .fmm file including retained history. A local recovery copy is also saved in this browser.

Heights are displayed at 1,000 m per height unit. The world starts with 1,024 × 1,024 samples; each expansion adds 256 samples to the selected borders.
