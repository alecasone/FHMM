FIELD NOTES

Your world starts here.

Paint in either view. The map and terrain update together.

Raise / Lower
Drag to build or carve terrain. Blend applies one controlled layer per stroke; release to add another.
Buildup
Blend prevents overlapping dabs from stacking. Stroke height sets the depth at full strength. Additive keeps building while you drag or hold.
Flatten
Uses the height under the first click as its target.
Smooth
Soften ridges. Hold Shift while sculpting.
Meld · M
Use a broad round brush to break down peaks and fill gaps together. Size controls the blending reach; Strength controls the speed. Hold to continue melding. Shift temporarily uses fine Smooth.
Stamp
Place a single peak with your selected heightmap.
Roads
Choose Road, a surface, and a width. Drag a freehand route or straight line and release to paint. Optional roadbed leveling softens the cross-slope. Roads use the color layer, support undo and saving, and can be erased with Natural paint. Existing saved rivers remain visible.
Objects
Season and lifecycle apply to new placements and are stored per object. Saplings are smaller; winter deciduous trees and dead trees expose branches. O opens trees, shrubs and rocks. Scatter splatters a brush area; Detail places one object per click. Eraser or Shift deletes objects in the circle, with an optional selected-type filter. Size and variation control the models. Objects follow sculpted terrain and are saved with history.
Sun / rotation
Sun azimuth and height light both views. Rotate the 2D map or orbit the 3D view using the sliders in View rotation. Painting, panning, and zooming follow the rotated view.
3D distance
Open View & scale and adjust Render distance to load terrain around the focus. Lower values are faster; the maximum renders the entire map and grows with its borders. Object capacity is one object per map sample.
Side panels
Use the small edge arrows to hide or restore the left menu, right menu, or top bar. Click a section heading to fold it, or use Collapse all / Expand all. Panel choices and render distance are remembered in this browser. Undo/Redo stay above the world controls.
Move around
Space + drag pans. Right-drag orbits in 3D. Scroll zooms.
Brush size
[ and ] change the radius. Heightmaps retain their original pixel dimensions and bit precision. The size slider stops at the selected heightmap’s native resolution; switching to a smaller heightmap clamps the current size. Use native size maps about one source pixel to one terrain sample; smaller brushes have fewer terrain samples available. Rectangular maps keep their proportions. Circular brush falloff still softens the outer edges.
Scale grid
View & scale contains a checkbox for the grid in both views. Set Grid cell size to the number of terrain samples per cell. The 2D view combines fine cells when zoomed out and reports the visible spacing. Cursor scale reference adds a fixed-size person, house or tree at the cursor in both views. These preferences are remembered.
Brush preview
Turn on the translucent stamp footprint in both views. Preview opacity controls its visibility without changing paint strength. Its shape follows the selected heightmap and rotation.
History
Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo and redo. Click a history row to travel back.
Creative paint
Canyon / mesa uses elevation-based color bands. Edit all four colors, thickness, tilt and direction. Moss & lichen and Volcanic ash add terrain-aware color variation. These brushes paint the current terrain; repaint after sculpting to recalculate the pattern.
Water coloring
In Biomes, enable Water coloring mode and choose a tint, then paint ocean areas below sea level. Natural or Shift erases the tint. Water appearance on the right sets shallow/deep colors, the depth where water reaches its darkest color, and the transition curve. Both views follow terrain depth; settings and tints are saved with undo history.
Biomes
Pick from terrain selects existing biome or custom paint. Choose a custom color to paint it directly; biome matching is optional. Brush strength goes down to 0.1%. B paints the selected biome. Water paints a blue surface color for lakes or rivers. E or Shift restores natural colors. Snow and rock follow the terrain slope.
Reset
Reset views repairs the display. Reset world clears or restores terrain, rivers, paint and objects, with Undo support.
Save
Ctrl+S saves a .fmm file including retained history. A local recovery copy is also saved in this browser.

Heights are displayed at 1,000 m per height unit, with an elevation range of −1,000 to +5,000 m. The world starts with 1,024 × 1,024 samples; each expansion adds 256 samples to the selected borders.
